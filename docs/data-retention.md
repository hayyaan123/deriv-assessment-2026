# Data Retention & Deletion

## Standard Retention

Customer data is retained for as long as an account remains active. Inactive accounts
(no login or API activity for 12 months) are flagged for review and may be scheduled for
deletion after an additional 90-day notice period.

## Customer-Initiated Deletion

Customers can request deletion of their data at any time through the dashboard's
"Delete My Data" tool or by contacting support. Deletion requests are **not** processed
immediately — they are queued and executed within 30 days, in order to allow for fraud
review and to comply with financial record-keeping requirements in some jurisdictions.
During this 30-day window, the deletion can be cancelled by the customer.

## Backups

Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to an additional 60 days after the
30-day deletion window, after which backups are rotated and the data is unrecoverable.

## Legal Holds

Data subject to a legal hold or active investigation is exempt from standard deletion
timelines and will be retained until the hold is lifted.
