# Pricing & Plans

## Plan Tiers

The platform offers three plans:

- **Free** — $0/month. Includes 1,000 API requests/month, 1 webhook endpoint, and
  community support. Designed for evaluation, side projects, and early-stage startups
  testing the platform before committing to a paid plan.
- **Growth** — $49/month. Includes 100,000 API requests/month, 10 webhook endpoints,
  and email support with a 24-hour response target.
- **Enterprise** — Custom pricing. Includes unlimited API requests, dedicated support,
  and a custom SLA. Contact sales for a quote.

## Startup Program

Startups with fewer than 10 employees and under $1M in funding can apply for the Startup
Program, which provides Growth-tier features at no cost for the first 12 months. The Free
plan itself is already available to any developer, including startups, with no application
required.

## Billing

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance. Usage beyond your plan's request quota is billed
at $0.001 per additional request, up to a hard cap of 3x your plan's included quota.
